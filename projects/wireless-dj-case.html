Wireless DJ case

A weatherproof case rebuilt as one self-contained mobile DJ setup that cuts our setup time dramatically.

Hardware3D printingAudio

At a glance

Problem
Setting up a sound system for each event took too long.
Built with
A weatherproof case, a USB-powered sound board, a wireless transmitter and microphone, a USB power bank, and 3D-printed parts
Result
Setup is now: put the speakers on stands, plug them in, turn on the sound board.

Starting point

We already had a weatherproof case for carrying our small USB-powered sound board. I turned it into the whole rig.

What is inside

  • The sound board, a wireless audio transmitter and a wireless microphone with a 5V-to-3V converter.
  • A strip of LED lights.
  • A USB power bank that runs everything.
  • A 3D-printed mount that holds the transmitter and the power bank.

The connectors and cables

I 3D-printed low-profile XLR connector caps, wired the wireless microphone from a 1/4" TRS cable into XLR, and made a custom XLR-to-3.5mm TRS cable to plug in my laptop.

How it works at an event

The active speakers have their own wireless receivers, so my workflow is: put the speakers on stands, plug them in, turn on the sound board, and I am done. It is not an audiophile's perfect build, but it dramatically cuts setup time, and it does the job.

What is next

I want to add a small dedicated computer to run TriviaNight and Music Bingo from inside the case.
