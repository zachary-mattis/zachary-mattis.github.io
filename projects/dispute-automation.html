Dispute automation

A Python tool I built at Wheeler Fleet Solutions that took a dispute process from about 5 minutes per case to under 90 seconds and recovered more than $300,000.

Problem

The situation

At Wheeler Fleet Solutions, filing an Amazon dispute was a manual process that took about five minutes for each case. Funds were at risk of expiring before all the cases could be filed.

What I built

I taught myself Python for this. The tool has three parts: a clipboard listener, an EDI file parser and image annotation. Together they brought each case down to under 90 seconds.

The result

  • More than 3,000 disputes filed in six weeks.
  • More than $300,000 recovered in funds that were about to expire.
  • From about five minutes per case to under 90 seconds.
