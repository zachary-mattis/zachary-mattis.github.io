The "broken" saw that became a projector power supply

Zachary Mattis
November 18, 2026

I thought I had broken my Craftsman V20 circular saw, because the motor would not spin. It turned out the motor had simply worked itself out of its bearing, which means the saw could probably have been fixed. By then I had already bought other tools.

A second life for the board

Instead of letting the saw sit, I pulled the battery management and tool power board out of it and used it to deliver a steady 18 to 20 volts to the projector we use at our events. I 3D modeled a case to hold the board, and replaced the switch with rails that accept the battery. A power line runs from there to the projector.

How it works out

With a 6Ah battery I get roughly 2.5 to 3 hours of run time, which covers most of our gigs. Paired with a wireless HDMI dongle, the projector is completely wireless.

What I took from it

Look at what is actually wrong before you replace something. I will do that sooner next time. The board turned out to be useful either way, and the full build is on the projector power supply page.
