Hello, and what this site is

Zachary Mattis
November 18, 2026

I am Zachary, a cybersecurity student about to graduate from Penn State, and this is my corner of the internet. I have been doing IT support for people around Stoystown, PA for years, and I like building things that make my own life, and other people's work, easier.

The projects section holds the finished write-ups: the apps I wrote for the trivia and music bingo nights at Ponfeigh Distillery in Somerset, the WaveForm app for Miller Electric and Pool Supply, the dispute automation I built at Wheeler Fleet Solutions, my home server, and the hardware that makes our events easy to set up.

What goes in the blog

The project pages are the tidy version. The blog is for the rest: problems I ran into, things I would do differently, and what I am learning along the way as I finish my degree and work toward Security+.

What I am looking for

I finish my B.A.Sc. in Cybersecurity Analytics and Operations in December 2026, and I am looking for an entry-level security analyst or IT support role. If you are hiring, or just want to talk shop, my email and LinkedIn are at the bottom of every page.
